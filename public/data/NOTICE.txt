The upstream license is GPL-3.0-or-later; its complete text is in
UMA-TOOLS-LICENSE.txt. Preserve that license and upstream attribution when
redistributing this dataset. Contributors include TheCing, kachi, Jecht,
alpha123 and pecan. simulator-skill-ids.json contains IDs from the same snapshot.

metadata.json and course-geometry.json contain selected UM:PD reference facts
and numeric course paths. No complete game databases, bundles or account data
are included. Game materials remain with their original rights holders.
The application license does not grant rights to those materials.

Missing names and skill definitions use GameTora's public reference catalogs.
Global definitions take priority over JP fallbacks. GameTora and its
contributors are credited for community names and translations. Source URLs,
revisions and hashes are recorded in the JSON provenance fields.
