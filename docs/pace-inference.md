# Position-keeping replay candidates

The supported Global replay layout records positions, actual velocity, HP, rushed state and blocking; it does not record PU/PD/SU/OT mode, pacemaker binding, entry lottery, exit threshold or cooldown. These modes remain reference-model candidates with `evidence: estimated`, not decoded flags or native execution traces. No candidate means unassessed, not mode off.

Each sample bracket is integrated from the observed self speed toward the no-mode target and toward each position-legal mode target. Resolved current-speed modifiers are removed from the observed speed first. Target-skill offsets, terrain, and a downhill bonus already confirmed by HP are inside every hypothesis, so they do not become a pace mode by themselves. A mode is kept only when its integral meets the next recorded speed more closely than the no-mode integral. The speed change inside the bracket does not widen the no-mode window. A climb that has not reached the raised ceiling is remembered as an onset and is not labeled until a later sample gets there. Unresolved scaling, masks, timing, stat/lane effects, instantaneous HP brackets, start dash, blocking and rushed/competition windows remain excluded. On a downhill, a surplus no larger than the hill bonus is left unlabeled while HP for that bracket is unusable.

Speed Up uses the overall lead and a nearby second runner. Overtake uses a rival of the **same effective strategy**, keeping normal Front and Runaway distinct; an equipped guaranteed Runaway skill or recorded Runaway activation can establish that distinction. A Front runner leading its strategy but behind a later-strategy runner is not automatically Overtake: the possible Pace Up Ex situation remains unmodeled. A contiguous Overtake candidate can remain compatible after moving into the lead until the same-strategy lead exceeds the reference exit gap; it is not relabeled Speed Up merely because the global gap becomes zero.

Nonfront pacemaker selection after the 1.5-anniversary changes is not established by the inspected evidence. The leading runner of the most forward strategy supplies an explicitly labeled gap proxy. PU/PD entry thresholds are not reapplied as exit thresholds: a contiguous candidate can remain compatible inside the min/max exit band when its velocity/HP evidence still supports it. Mid-race PD uses the reference 0.945 coefficient and reduced exit band; opening PD uses 0.915. Positive target/current speed skills exclude PD as specified by the reference mechanics. Sampled candidates do not reconstruct hidden entry rolls, one-section timers or cooldowns.

Only exactly contiguous qualifying brackets are merged. No missing/censored gap is bridged. A changed mode coefficient creates a separate numeric interval. Candidate fragmentation caused by unrecorded transitions, recovery brackets, unknown effects or coarse samples remains visible; fewer fragments are not by themselves evidence of greater correctness.
